# Retail Media Intelligence

## Background

MarMart is an ecommerce retailer that allows brands to advertise products within its search results.

Advertisers pay MarMart when shoppers click sponsored products.

MarMart wants to grow advertising revenue while ensuring advertisers receive good returns and shoppers continue receiving relevant search results.

## Business Problem

How can MarMart improve advertiser ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) while maintaining retailer revenue and a good shopping experience?


## Stakeholders

- Retail Media Product Manager
- Advertisers 
- Sales Team
